Ruins of St. Paul's

Historical Site

The Ruins of St. Paul’s are one of Macau’s most famous historical landmarks. What remains today is the grand stone façade of St. Paul’s Church, which was built in the 17th century and later destroyed by fire. The detailed carvings and historical architecture make the site an important part of Macau’s heritage. Visitors can explore the ruins, take photographs, and learn about the area’s history while walking through the historic center of Macau.
