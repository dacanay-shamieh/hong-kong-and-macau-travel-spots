Ngong Ping 360 Cable Car

Scenic Attraction

Ngong Ping 360 Cable Car is a scenic attraction in Hong Kong that offers visitors a beautiful aerial view of Lantau Island. The cable car travels between Tung Chung and Ngong Ping, passing over mountains, greenery, and the surrounding landscape. It is a great choice for travelers who enjoy sightseeing and taking in panoramic views. The journey also provides a convenient way to reach attractions such as the Big Buddha and Ngong Ping Village.
