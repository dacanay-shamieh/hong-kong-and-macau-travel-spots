Big Buddha

Religious Site

The Big Buddha, also known as Tian Tan Buddha, is one of Hong Kong’s most recognizable landmarks. Located on Lantau Island near Ngong Ping, the large bronze statue sits on a hilltop surrounded by mountains and peaceful scenery. Visitors can climb the steps leading to the statue and enjoy the impressive views of the surrounding area. It is a meaningful destination for those interested in Hong Kong’s culture, architecture, and religious heritage.
